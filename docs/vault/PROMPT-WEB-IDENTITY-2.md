## 1. What was decided (docs/GATES.md, "Decided on 2026-10-08 (Rodrigo)")

**IDENTITY-2.** "Honey on night" replaces "The Open Joint". One brand colour, **honey `#F5A83A`**, with **ink `#15161C` text on it in both modes** (white on honey is banned, 2.0:1). The dark ground is a cool near-black (**night `#0C0D12`**, cards `#13151C`, raised `#1A1D26`, hairline `#262A36`, control edge `#363B4B`, text `#F3F1EC`, muted `#9A9DAD`). The light ground is warm paper (**`#F7F5F0`**, white cards, wells `#EFECE5`, hairline `#E3DFD6`, control edge `#C9C4B9`, ink `#15161C`, muted `#6A6D78`). Direction colours, always with a word and a square glyph: **leaf** `#3FC47C` / `#1B7F45` (on track, positive deltas), **clay** `#F0703A` / `#A8481A` (watch), **madder** `#EF5A6F` / `#B52F44` (off track, negative deltas, destructive); each with a 14% tint fill for pills and selected rows. **Chalk** `#78B4E8` / `#2A73B0` is a line only: the focus ring, "today" lines, guides. Honey as text or link on light grounds is **honey-l `#A8640A`**. Hover `#E99C2E`, pressed `#D9881B`. Gradients only as light (a honey glow behind heroes, a honey fill under the base-case curve); no two-hue gradients, no glass, no blur. Type: **Inter Tight 600** (−0.02em) for display and big numbers, **Inter** 400/500/600 for UI, **IBM Plex Mono** for provenance and code. **No serif anywhere in the product.** Shape: cards 10 px, buttons/inputs/popovers 8 px, tags 6 px, chips and status badges pills, app tiles 16 px; the composer stays 20 px with its round send button; token identification marks stay round (Thom, Oct 7). No shadows except popovers (`0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.35)`).

**LOGO-2.** The mark is "the face": a honey tile (radius 7 at 32 px), the tenon end cut into it (18 × 12, radius 2) in night on dark grounds and in ink elsewhere, and a square honey pin (5 × 5, radius 1) set toward the end. Three cuts: 32 master (≥ 28 px), 24 hint (20–27 px), 16 small (12–19 px). Wordmark `tenonfi` in Inter Tight 600, −2% tracking, lowercase; fallback `tenon`. The provenance pin is the same tenon end in line mode with a **square** pin.

**Unchanged:** every product rule. The pin on every figure. `MOCK-QUIET` (no boxed MOCK word; the hatched edge, the quiet "Sample figures" line, the hatched glyph named "sample figure"). The disclaimer. No return promises. The brand recedes in the embed. No Japanese clichés, nothing like Teiten.
