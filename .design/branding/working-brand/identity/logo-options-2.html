Identity · logo options, round 2 · 2026-10-08 · drawn on the accepted palette

Five marks on honey and night

Brief for every mark: say cut to fit and every joint shown, hold at 16 px, recede to a "Powered by" credit, carry "Bearing" without a second brand, and be built from honey, the ground and the ground's text colour only. Wordmark: tenonfi, lowercase, Inter Tight 600, −2% tracking. Each option is shown at 96 / 48 / 32 / 16 on night and on paper, as the primary lockup, as "tenonfi Bearing", as app tiles, and as the partner credit. Round 1's three directions are retired.

1 · The face end view of the pinned through-tenon

ConceptWhat you see on the outside of a post where a through-tenon comes out: the end grain, and the pin that holds it. A tile, the tenon end cut into it, the pin set toward the end as a draw-bore pin is.

Says"Held, and you can see what holds it." The pin is the only detail, so it is the provenance pin's parent.

RiskThree nested shapes can read as a card with a dot or a toggle. The tile saves it in app contexts; alone on a page it is quiet. Best as the small cut of option 3.

2 · A moment before it locks mortise block, tenon approaching

ConceptThe governing idea of the brief, finally drawn: the post with its mortise cut, the rail with its tenon, a gap between them. Two members in two tones. The mortise is negative space, so the ground is part of the mark.

Says"Cut for each other." The most literal "made to measure". The gap is the promise: it fits, you can see it will.

RiskA socket and a plug. That is the right meaning, but a USB reading is possible at 16 px. The two tones and the pin hole hold it above icon level at 32 and up.

3 · The joint axonometric, two species, pinned

recommended, with 1 as its small cut

ConceptA post in one species with the tenon end of a rail coming through its side in the other, held by a pin of the first. Three flat tones per member, no gradient, no outline. It is the master photograph reduced to its structure.

Says"A real joint, and you can see through it." It is the only option that reads as wood and as joinery without being told. Honey post, pale tenon on night; the tenon goes ink on paper, so the two temperatures of the identity are in the mark.

RiskSix faces at 16 px is too many, so the small cut drops the pin and the side faces. Isometric marks are common in infra brands (Vercel's triangle, Linear's lines), but none of them draws a joint.

4 · The t as a joint lettermark, two members half-lapped

ConceptA lowercase t built from two members: the stem in honey, the crossbar in the ground's text colour, lapped where they cross, with the t's foot so it never reads as a cross.

Says"tenon", and that the name is made of parts. A letter, so it carries the brand on its own without the wordmark.

RiskA lettermark says the least about fit or provenance. Letter t marks are plentiful (Tumblr, Trello's old mark, Typeform). It stands apart only through the two tones.

5 · The pin on the i wordmark-led; the icon is the i

ConceptNo symbol. The wordmark carries the brand, and the one touch of colour is the dot of the i, drawn as a square pin in honey. The icon is that i: a stem in the text colour and the pin above it.

Says"Said, and pinned." The lightest system: the brand recedes to a word, which suits the embed.

RiskThe i alone reads as an information icon. And the wordmark lives in Inter Tight, which half the category also uses, so it is ownable only by the pin. Works well as the companion wordmark to options 2 or 3.

Recommendation

Option 3 as the mark, option 1 as its small cut, and the plain wordmark. Three is the only drawing that says "wood joint" unprompted, and it puts both temperatures of the new identity in one shape: honey post, pale tenon on night, ink tenon on paper. One is literally the end of that tenon seen from outside, so the favicon and the credit stay the same joint at a size where the axonometric cannot survive. The provenance pin inherits the square pin from both. Two is the strongest story and the runner-up; take it if you want the "fit" told literally and accept the plug reading at 16 px. Four and five are there to show the alternatives were drawn, not to compete.
