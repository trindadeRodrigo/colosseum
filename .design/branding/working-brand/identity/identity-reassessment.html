Identity · re-assessment · 2026-10-08 · proposal, not a decision

Why the brand reads dead, and what to change

Benchmark: Meteora (measured on 2026-10-08: ground #0D0D13, surface #12121C, text #F9F9FB, muted #9595B2, accent #F54B00 with 16% tints, second accent #6E45FF, deltas #24C98D / #F04438, Inter, pills and 4–8 px radii, the product in the first viewport). This sheet keeps the idea the strategy committed to, made to measure, every joint shown, and replaces the material register that was built on it. Every sample below is the same screen in the current system and in the proposed one.

1 · The diagnosis

The current palette has no chroma. Its most saturated brand colour, hardwood, sits at OKLCH chroma 0.06. Meteora's accent sits at about 0.21. The two "species" are two browns on a brown-black, which is wood panelling, not wood in light. The rules then banned everything that makes a screen feel alive: blue, gradients, glow, green and red, rounded corners, a saturated accent, white. And the governing photograph was a studio shot of wood in the dark, so the palette was sampled from shadow. The daylight photograph already in the prototype has honey, amber, leaf green and sky in it.

2 · The palette: light on wood, not wood in the dark

One brand colour, honey: fresh-cut hinoki in sunlight. It is the only warm hue in the chrome, so it owns attention. The ground goes cool (a trace of blue in the black, as Meteora's) so the warm colour glows instead of sinking. Direction colours come from the daylight photograph: leaf from the forest in the window, madder for off track, clay for watch. Chalk is the carpenter's snap line, a pale blue used only for guides, selection and focus, never as a brand colour. Wood itself leaves the UI chrome and lives in imagery and the logo.

The button rule that makes it ours. Honey is light, so the primary button is always ink on honey, in both modes. Meteora is white on orange, Jupiter is black on lime, Binance is black on yellow. Black on honey next to a cool black ground, with wood photography, is a combination nobody on Solana holds. Honey text on white needs the deeper honey-l; honey itself is for fills, bars, the pin and large type only.

Newsreader as a product face. It gave the dead screens their funeral register. If a serif survives at all it is one line on the marketing site, never in the app, Bearing or the embed. Alternative pair if Inter feels too generic: Geist + Geist Mono, same rules.
ShapeRadius 10 px cards, 8 px controls, pills for chips and tags, the composer stays 20 px. The "square only" rule is dropped: joinery is carried by construction motifs (dimension lines, exploded views, the pin), not by sharp corners.
LightGradients are allowed as light, never as two hues: a honey radial at 14–22% behind heroes and on primary hover, a honey fill under the base-case curve. No glass, no blur, no neon glow.
TintsEvery state colour has a 14% tint for fills (selected rows, chips, badges). This is what makes Meteora read alive at density: colour sits in surfaces, not only in text.
ImageryWood in daylight replaces wood in the studio. Governing image: the timber interior with the forest in the window (already in the prototype as the closing image). 7 · What this changes in the brand documents

Rules dropped

  • Wood is the only colour. Replaced by one brand colour plus direction colours, with wood in imagery.
  • No blue anywhere. Chalk is allowed for guides, focus and selection lines. Still no blue brand, no blue gradients.
  • No gradients, no glow. Gradients as light are allowed; two-hue gradients, glass and neon stay out.
  • Square only, 2 px radius. Replaced by the shape row above.
  • No pure white. White cards on warm paper are allowed.
  • Earth-pigment status colours. Replaced by leaf, clay and madder at full saturation, still always with a word and a shape.
  • Newsreader as the answer voice. Dropped from product surfaces.

Rules kept

  • Made to measure, every joint shown. Sage × Caregiver. The voice.
  • Provenance pin, hatch + MOCK, "test network", the disclaimer, no return promises, status as word + shape + colour.
  • No Japanese clichés. Nothing that reads as Teiten. No mascots, no rockets, no leaderboards.
  • The brand recedes in the partner embed.
  • Light and dark are peers. Dark is native for Bearing and the video, light for docs, the plan and the embed.

Then

On a yes to a hue and this sheet: rewrite the identity colour and typography chunks, regenerate tokens, STYLE.md and the component specs from the new values (the GSP guidelines phase does that part well), reskin the landing prototype, and draw the logo variations on the new palette.
